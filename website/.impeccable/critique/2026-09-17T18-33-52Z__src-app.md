## Design specificity verdict

Grounded at the level of evidence; category-interchangeable at the level of structure.

Genuinely this product: the stage reproduces real ISPer chrome with a live status label moving pronto → ouvindo → gravando reunião; /download is built around the real 9,8 MB vs 422,8 MB asymmetry with real SHA-256s; the SmartScreen block quotes the literal Windows strings in order; the benchmark panel refuses to plot a time axis it does not have.

Interchangeable: the entire skeleton. Hero split → icon trust strip → 2x2 bento of icon-chip cards → library screenshot → comparison table → shortcut section → FAQ accordion → final CTA. Measured structural sameness: seven display headings, six of them H2s at exactly 65.6px, with a type scale of 83 / 65.6 / 21.6 / 16 — a 3x gap with nothing in the middle. One rhetorical volume repeated six times.

The only visual statement of it is prose inside a collapsed details element at 78% scroll depth. A single honest data-flow picture would out-persuade the entire bento grid and could not be pasted onto another product.

## What is working

1. The benchmark panel: two marks, a hatched band for the stated range, a provenance chip on every mark, restated as a table with a "Natureza do dado" column. The visual form cannot express more certainty than the data supports, so the heading's promise is structurally guaranteed rather than asserted.
2. The stage's surrender rule: scroll drives the demo, but any deliberate click or keypress ends the sequence permanently. It resolves the usual scrollytelling conflict — the page teaches, then gets out of the way and never reclaims control.
3. Both installer variants staying on screen, selection raising a card and adding a filled check rather than erasing the alternative.

## Priority issues

### [P1] The page contradicts itself about the product's most repeated interaction
The hero stage renders Ctrl+Shift+Espaço labelled "Atalho configurável"; the shortcut section renders Ctrl+Alt+Espaço labelled "O padrão é Ctrl + Alt + Espaço". content/docs/solucao-de-problemas/atalhos.md is unambiguous: Ctrl+Alt+Espaço is the default and Ctrl+Shift+Espaço is the fallback. A portal whose pitch is verifiability shows the wrong shortcut in its own demo.
Fix: render the default in the stage and label the alternates, sourced from one constant shared with the shortcut section.

### [P1] Two of four header nav items are broken from every page except /
Measured: from /docs/, clicking "Recursos" navigates to /#recursos and lands at scrollY 0 while #recursos sits 1211px down. Cause: smooth-scroll.tsx forces lenis.scrollTo(0, { immediate: true, force: true }) on every pathname change, overriding the hash. The address bar says #recursos, the reader is at the top of the hero, and nothing signals that anything went wrong.
Fix: skip the reset when a hash is present and scroll to it after the route commits.

### [P1] The most persuasive element is the least sourced, and half of it is invisible on a phone
.comparison-table makes 15 claims about "API de transcrição" and "Notetaker corporativo" with zero provenance, two sections below the panel that establishes the site's evidentiary standard, against PRODUCT.md's traceability boundary. At 390px the wrap is 346px against a 578px table: columns 3 and 4 are cut with no fade, no visible scrollbar and no hint.
Fix: a provenance line under the caption in the CENÁRIO vocabulary; the edge mask DESIGN.md already specifies for overflowing rails; below 640px restack as per-criterion cards.

### [P2] /download puts the fold on a headline and the reassurance after the action
At 1512x900 the primary download link starts at y=862 with 38px visible; the SmartScreen notice that pre-empts the scariest moment begins at y≈1459, below the button that triggers it. The header block's right half is empty.
Fix: two-column header, lifting the variant grid to ~y=380 and the download row to ~y=640; move the SmartScreen notice above or beside the download row.

### [P2] role="tablist" without its keyboard contract, twice, plus two inert buttons in the hero
All four [role="tab"] elements have tabindex null and neither component has a key handler, so ARIA promises roving focus and arrow keys that do not exist. Separately .app-titlebar renders real buttons labelled Biblioteca and Configurações with no onClick — 2 of the 6 hero tab stops do nothing.
Fix: roving tabindex plus arrow/Home/End on both tablists; make the titlebar chrome aria-hidden or give it real destinations.
